  1. 01Field-wide · big companies

    Nvidia's board is reportedly not considering Jensen Huang's return, on the eve of its results

    What happenedOn 16 November, Reuters and CNBC reported, citing people familiar with the matter, that Nvidia's board is not weighing a return of Jensen Huang, who stepped down as CEO on 12 November. A public petition for his return passed 180,000 signatures, and President Trump said he would not mind seeing him back. Nvidia's shares fell more than 4% on Monday, and the company reports its third-quarter results on 17 November after US markets close, its first report under interim CEO Colette Kress.

    Why it mattersNvidia supplies most of the hardware behind AI research and cloud services, so instability at the top can affect prices, supply and long-term plans for many universities and companies. It is also a clear case study in corporate governance and key-person risk.

    Note: the board has made no public statement; the reports rely on anonymous sources. The petition's numbers of employees and shareholders are self-reported and not verified.

    Source: Reuters via BNN Bloomberg · 16–17 Nov 2026, and CNBC · 16 Nov 2026

  2. 02Artificial intelligence · governance

    Anthropic's IPO filing shows how a trust is meant to protect its mission, and experts doubt how far it goes

    What happenedAnthropic's amended IPO filing (10 November) describes its governance as a public benefit corporation: a Long-Term Benefit Trust of five independent trustees holds a special share that lets it elect a majority of the board, while the founders hold 50.1% of general voting power. Anthropic also put shares worth 5% of the company into a public benefit fund. Experts quoted by AP on 16 November say the trust cannot steer daily decisions and may find it hard to remove directors in a real conflict between profit and mission.

    Why it mattersThis will be one of the largest tests of whether a legal structure can keep an AI developer focused on safety under market pressure, which is useful material for courses on AI governance and ethics.

    Note: Bloomberg reported that the offer was about six to seven times oversubscribed; the price ($70–78 range) is expected on 18 November, with trading on Nasdaq from 19 November. These details are from reports and the filing, not final.

    Source: AP via The Philadelphia Inquirer · 16 Nov 2026, and Unite.AI · 16 Nov 2026

  3. 03Field-wide · education

    International master's students in the US fall by 22%, and new students by almost 18%

    What happenedThe Open Doors 2026 report from IIE and the US State Department, published on 16 November, counts about 1.15 million international students in 2025–26, down 2.7%. Master's students fell 22.4% and new students 17.7%, while doctoral students rose 3.1%. Math and computer science fell 6.3%. A separate IIE snapshot for fall 2026 shows a further 9% drop at 809 responding colleges, which mostly blame visa problems and travel restrictions.

    Why it mattersMany information science and information systems master's programs depend on international students, so this directly affects enrolment, budgets and the future talent pipeline.

    Note: the fall 2026 figures are from a survey of responding institutions, not a full national count.

    Source: IIE Open Doors press release · 16 Nov 2026, and Higher Ed Dive · 16 Nov 2026

  4. 04Field-wide · job market

    Goldman Sachs: the AI layoff wave is "delayed, not cancelled"

    What happenedGoldman Sachs' 2027 US outlook, reported by Fortune on 16 November, expects one more year of a "low-hire, low-fire" job market, with AI replacing more jobs in later years. It forecasts 2.4% US growth in 2027 and no recession. An earlier Goldman note estimated that up to 9% of workers could be displaced during the AI transition.

    Why it mattersLow hiring hits graduates first: Stanford researchers report that employment of 22–25-year-olds in the most AI-exposed jobs is about 19% below the expected level. This matters for advising students in information systems careers.

    Note: these are forecasts, which Goldman itself calls a best guess; other researchers argue AI will spread more slowly, like earlier technologies.

    Source: Fortune · 16 Nov 2026, and CASRAI · 17 Nov 2026

  5. 05Information systems · health data & privacy

    Apple and OpenEvidence start a study of how up to 200,000 people use AI for health questions

    What happenedOn 15 November, Apple launched the Apple Health AI Usage Study, a research app built with OpenEvidence, a clinical AI service that answers from medical sources. Adults aged 21 and over in the US can ask health questions and rate the answers. Participants are invited to share their Apple Health (HealthKit) data, so the researchers can see whether personal data makes answers more relevant.

    Why it mattersIt is a large, real-world study of information seeking with AI in a sensitive area, and it raises clear questions about consent, data minimisation and how personal health data is used to personalise answers.

    Note: the reports we found give few details on data retention or oversight; check the study's own consent terms.

    Source: TechCrunch · 15 Nov 2026, and PYMNTS · 16 Nov 2026

  6. 06Artificial intelligence · new models

    Tencent releases Hy4 flash, a very cheap model with a one-million-token context

    What happenedOn 16 November, Tencent made Hy4 flash available on Tencent Cloud, two weeks after its full Hy4 model. It supports image understanding, function calling and reasoning, with a one-million-token context. The list price is about $0.07 per million input tokens and $0.28 per million output tokens, more than ten times cheaper than full Hy4, and the API accepts OpenAI- and Anthropic-style requests.

    Why it mattersFalling prices for long-context models make it cheaper to process large document collections, for example for retrieval and classification projects in research and teaching.

    Note: Tencent's claim of a 95% cost cut comes from its own internal tests; we found no independent quality benchmark.

    Source: TechNode · 16 Nov 2026, and CASRAI · 16 Nov 2026

  7. 07Information systems · standards

    Poppy 1.0: a new standard for how personal AI agents talk to businesses, switched on across Shopify

    What happenedOn 13 November, Sierra published version 1.0 of its Personal Agent Protocol ("Poppy"). It tells a visiting AI agent who it is acting for and what it may do, using existing standards such as MCP, OpenAPI and OAuth, so customers control the agent's access. Shopify enabled it for every store, Meta's Muse agent checks for it first on every site, and more than 100 companies are design partners.

    Why it mattersAgents are becoming a new kind of "user" of information systems. Shared rules for identity, permissions and consent are what will decide whether this is safe and interoperable, and the opposite approach is also visible: United Airlines has blocked unauthorised booking agents.

    Note: despite the 1.0 name, the specification is still marked as a draft and may change; competing efforts exist from Visa, Mastercard and Amazon.

    Source: Sierra blog · 13 Nov 2026, and The Next Web · 13 Nov 2026

  2. ۰۲هوش مصنوعی · حکمرانی

    پروندهٔ عرضهٔ سهام Anthropic نشان می‌دهد یک تراست چطور باید از مأموریت شرکت محافظت کند؛ کارشناسان دربارهٔ حدود آن تردید دارند

    چه اتفاقی افتادپروندهٔ اصلاح‌شدهٔ عرضهٔ عمومی سهام Anthropic (۱۰ نوامبر) ساختار حکمرانی آن را به‌عنوان یک شرکت عام‌المنفعه (public benefit corporation) شرح می‌دهد: یک Long-Term Benefit Trust با پنج عضو مستقل یک سهم ویژه دارد که به آن اجازه می‌دهد اکثریت هیئت‌مدیره را انتخاب کند، در حالی که بنیان‌گذاران ۵۰٫۱ درصد حق رأی عمومی را در اختیار دارند. Anthropic همچنین سهامی معادل ۵ درصد شرکت را به یک صندوق عام‌المنفعه داده است. کارشناسانی که AP در ۱۶ نوامبر از آن‌ها نقل کرده، می‌گویند این تراست نمی‌تواند تصمیم‌های روزمره را هدایت کند و در تعارض واقعی میان سود و مأموریت شاید به‌سختی بتواند مدیران را برکنار کند.

    چرا مهم استاین یکی از بزرگ‌ترین آزمون‌ها برای این پرسش است که آیا یک ساختار حقوقی می‌تواند یک توسعه‌دهندهٔ هوش مصنوعی را زیر فشار بازار به ایمنی پایبند نگه دارد؛ موضوعی مفید برای درس‌های حکمرانی و اخلاق هوش مصنوعی.

    توجه: Bloomberg گزارش داده که تقاضا برای سهام حدود شش تا هفت برابر عرضه بوده است؛ قیمت (در بازهٔ ۷۰ تا ۷۸ دلار) قرار است در ۱۸ نوامبر تعیین شود و معاملات در Nasdaq از ۱۹ نوامبر آغاز شود. این جزئیات از گزارش‌ها و پرونده آمده و هنوز نهایی نیست.

    منبع: AP به نقل از The Philadelphia Inquirer · ۱۶ نوامبر ۲۰۲۶، و Unite.AI · ۱۶ نوامبر ۲۰۲۶

    Poppy 1.0: استانداردی تازه برای گفت‌وگوی عامل‌های شخصی هوش مصنوعی با کسب‌وکارها، فعال در همهٔ فروشگاه‌های Shopify
